Andrejs Zavadskis: Commercial, Advertising, Editorial and Portrait Photographer — Forbes USA, Dakar Rally and Five Gumball 3000s

European photographer based in Riga, Latvia — a commercial, advertising, editorial and portrait photographer from the Baltic States who has worked in more than fifty countries for over twenty years. The assignment changes. The method does not: arrive early, read the light, and be ready when the moment happens.

Forbes USA calls when a founder or a billionaire has almost no time, and one portrait has to carry the story. Advertising and commercial briefs ask for the same discipline in a different room: a product, a campaign, a face that has to sell the idea. Editorial work takes him out of the studio and into the weather. The Dakar Rally sends a truck through a wall of sand and does not offer a second pass. The Gumball 3000 — five times now — puts him inside the convoy rather than behind a barrier.

This is not a biography. It is a working record of the man who keeps getting the call: Andrejs Zavadskis, often called Latvia's most famous photographer.

Faces, campaigns and covers

Clients include Forbes USA, Nike, airBaltic, LMT, Rimi, Nordea, Swedbank and Tele2. Whether the job is a magazine cover, an advertising campaign or a commercial portrait, the hard part is the same: a powerful person, almost no time, and one frame that has to hold the headline.

  • Thomas Plantenga, Vinted — Forbes USA, April 2024, "Riches from Rags".
  • Paweł Chudziński, Point Nine Capital — the "Europe's Top VC" cover, also used for the Midas List Europe 2024.
  • Samir Mane — Albania's first billionaire, April 2025.
  • Remon Vos, CTP — photographed in Prague; now part of The Forbes Collection on Getty Images.
  • Alex Bouzari, DDN — August 2026.

The same portraits were later used by Forbes México, Forbes Česko, Forbes Italia and Forbes España.

Sand, ice and a summit

In January 2020 he photographed the Dakar Rally — trucks the size of houses, heat, and dust that swallows the road. Five Gumball 3000 rallies followed, with his pictures printed in the official Gumball 3000 photo book. Porsche cars on frozen lakes in Swedish Lapland. In 2022 he climbed Kilimanjaro on foot to Uhuru Peak, 5,895 metres, camera still in his hands.

Motion has one rule: choose the place early, trust the light, and let the shutter finish the thought.

During the Dakar Rally in Saudi Arabia in January 2020, a rider sits on quad bike number 277, which is missing a wheel, at a rally service area. Zavadskis photographed the rally that year — heat, dust, and machines that do not offer a second pass if a frame is missed. Photo: Andrejs Zavadskis.

Dust, a film set, a different kind of dark

Burning Man in Nevada, where the dust becomes a filter and the night becomes a studio. In 2021, a quieter assignment: behind-the-scenes stills for the Danish feature Hvidstengruppen II, partly filmed in Latvia. The Latvian Ministry of Culture used one of his frames in its official announcement.

At Burning Man in Nevada's Black Rock Desert, a costumed participant stands on a large sculpted hare with antlers — one of the event's temporary art installations. The desert light and fine dust of the playa are the conditions Zavadskis works in there. Photo: Andrejs Zavadskis.

Playboy Latvia, 2010–2014

The Latvian Playboy lasted four years and fifty issues. Twenty-two of them were photographed by Andrejs Zavadskis — more than any other photographer who worked for the magazine. His May 2014 cover, Nr. 44, was singer Lauris Reiniks — the first man ever to appear on a Playboy Latvia cover, a frame later noticed by US Playboy's official Twitter. One of his Latvian models was later chosen by a foreign Playboy edition, a first for the Latvian magazine, and three of his models were eventually published abroad. Lingita Lina Bopulu went to Playmate of the Year in Manila and came home "Miss Talent"; a year later Zane Helda finished third in the world in the Philippines. Read the story of the 22 issues.

Where it started

2004: a photograph of fireworks over the Riga Radio and Television Tower takes first prize in a competition run by the Latvian State Radio and Television Centre. Then the Riga Photo Club, and in 2008 a Master Craftsman's Diploma from the Latvian Chamber of Crafts. 2009: a cover and feature in the UK magazine South Wales YOURWEDDING, and Wedding Photographer of the Year from the Latvian Wedding Photographers Association. March 2025: third place in the Fstoppers international contest "This Was Difficult!".

Today

One photo studio in Riga. Talks at Riga Photo Show. A photography blog running since 2008. Read the latest at blog.zavadskis.eu. YouTube gear reviews that come with the RAW files. More than 7,000 volunteer photographs for Latvia's 2025 School Youth Song and Dance Festival. And since 2026, ProPortfolio.pro — a free platform he founded for photographers, videographers, models, stylists and studios.
